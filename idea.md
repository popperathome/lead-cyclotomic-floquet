# Cyclotomic spectra organize Floquet phases and time-crystal subharmonics

**Status:** ⏳ QUEUED

## Claim

The hierarchy S¹ ⊃ C_p ⊃ C_{p²} ⊃ … of cyclotomic homotopy theory
(Nikolaus–Scholze, arXiv:1707.01799) is the right organizing structure for
periodically driven quantum systems: periodic time translation (Floquet),
period multiplication, and prime-power subharmonic responses of discrete time
crystals (Else–Bauer–Nayak, PRX 7, 011026) — better matched than ordinary
equivariant K-theory. Honest framing from conception: the obstruction is
immediate and known — no meaningful cyclotomic spectrum has been constructed
from a Floquet operator algebra or category of phases; until one exists the
resemblance is structural, not mathematical. Keyword searches found essentially
no existing bridge. A true moonshot.

## Decisive test

Obstruction-first construction attempt in the minimal case (theory work plus a
small computation):

1. Take the simplest Floquet system class (driven qudit; crossed product of
   the observable algebra by ℤ time-translation). Attempt the natural
   candidate: THH of the Floquet crossed-product algebra, and ask whether it
   carries the required Frobenius-type C_p structure in a way that sees the
   drive.
2. Concrete discriminating check: for a period-doubling discrete time crystal
   versus an undriven/trivial Floquet system, does the candidate's C₂-fixed
   or Frobenius data differ? Compare whatever invariant emerges against the
   known Floquet-SPT classification of Else et al.\ in the same setting.
3. If any nontrivial match appears, only then formalize the dictionary
   (period multiplication ↔ cyclotomic structure maps).

## Kill criteria

- The natural candidate provably forgets the drive: THH (or any functorial
  variant attempted) of the Floquet algebra is independent of the Floquet
  operator within a phase class — no physical information enters the
  cyclotomic structure.
- The Frobenius maps admit no counterpart in the period-multiplication
  physics after an honest construction attempt — resemblance remains
  structural; record as PREMATURE/DIED depending on whether a concrete
  obstruction was identified.
- Literature collision: a Floquet–cyclotomic bridge already exists (search
  "cyclotomic spectra Floquet", "TC time crystal", "topological cyclic
  homology driven phases").
